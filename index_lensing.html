🌌 Gravitational Lensing Lab

This sandbox showcases general relativistic light deflection: rays passing near the singularity bend toward the event horizon, forming Einstein rings and gravitational distortion in 3D.

💡 Controls:
• Drag Black Hole with mouse in top 2D map
• Alt+Click anywhere to teleport singularity
• 'I' key cycles slider magnitude (0.01x - 100x)
• WASD: Move through all walls freely (WASD keys only)
